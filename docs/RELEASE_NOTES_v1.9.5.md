# v1.9.5 — a stalled step fails when its page is replaced

## Provider-compatibility patch / Provider 相容性補丁

Multi-AI Chat Desktop `v1.9.5` fails a step when that provider's page is replaced mid-turn. The step stops with an explanation. It no longer hangs through the 60-minute absolute cap, and a manual paste into the new page no longer extends the old wait.

Multi-AI Chat Desktop `v1.9.5` 會在 provider 的頁面於這一步中途被換掉時，讓步驟失敗並給出說明。它不會再掛到 60 分鐘的絕對上限，在新頁面裡手動貼上內容也不會把舊的等待再延長。

A Windows zh-TW Roundtable on published `v1.9.4` is the record of that hang. Meta completed three rounds as the prompt grew, then round 4 stalled. Round 1 sent 4,742 characters and reached `RESPONSE_DONE` at 676 characters in 26.0 s. Round 2 sent 8,811 characters and reached `RESPONSE_DONE` at 1,016 characters in 33.5 s. Round 3 sent 14,165 characters and reached `RESPONSE_DONE` at 1,222 characters in 47.0 s; that round spent roughly 35 s with the bridge marked degraded, and the outbox backed up to 8 entries before flushing all at once. Round 4 sent 21,233 characters at 05:01:13.858 on bootId `ts684yo5` and never completed. The last engine status from `ts684yo5` was 05:01:06.016, then 73.3 s of silence across the composer-fill window. The webview was replaced twice: `4vpb89uv` at 05:02:34.452, with a `logged_out` flap, and `d5yqanjo` at 05:02:40.646. The new page reported `thinking: true` at 05:03:20.667 because the owner pasted into the provider window by hand, trying to rescue the step. `src/workflow/` had no `bootId` awareness, so a waiter settled only on `RESPONSE_DONE`, supersede, or timeout. A new engine boots with `waitingForResponse = false`, which gates every capture path, so the replaced page could not finish the old step. `reportsProviderActivity()` re-armed the 10-minute inactivity timer on `thinking === true`, and that paste extended the hang up to the 60-minute absolute cap.

這份現場紀錄是已發布的 `v1.9.4`、Windows zh-TW 的一場 Roundtable。Meta 在提示越變越長時完成三輪，第四輪停住。第一輪送出 4,742 字，26.0 秒後以 676 字 `RESPONSE_DONE`。第二輪送出 8,811 字，33.5 秒後以 1,016 字完成。第三輪送出 14,165 字，47.0 秒後以 1,222 字完成；這一輪大約有 35 秒橋接被標成 degraded，outbox 堆到 8 筆才一次送出。第四輪在 05:01:13.858、bootId `ts684yo5` 送出 21,233 字，之後沒有完成。`ts684yo5` 最後一筆 engine 狀態是 05:01:06.016，接著整段填入輸入框的窗口靜默了 73.3 秒。Webview 接著被換了兩次：05:02:34.452 的 `4vpb89uv`，中間有一次 `logged_out` 抖動，以及 05:02:40.646 的 `d5yqanjo`。05:03:20.667 新頁面回報 `thinking: true`，是擁有者在 provider 視窗手動貼上內容，想把這一步救回來。當時 `src/workflow/` 不認識 `bootId`，等待只會在 `RESPONSE_DONE`、被較新的送出取代、或逾時才結束。新的 engine 啟動時 `waitingForResponse = false`，每條擷取路徑都先看這個旗標，換上的頁面無法完成舊步驟。`reportsProviderActivity()` 看到 `thinking === true` 會把 10 分鐘不活動計時重新裝上，那次手動貼上就把卡住的時間再延長，最後只會被 60 分鐘的絕對上限截斷。

[#108](https://github.com/teddashh/multi-ai-chat-desktop/pull/108) teaches the workflow to see that replacement. `pull.ts` exports `onProviderBootRotation()`, fired when a bootId differs from one already seen for that provider. `waitForResponse` rejects every waiter for that provider with `ProviderPageReloadedError` before the new status is published, so `thinking` from the new page cannot re-arm the inactivity timer. The first boot the host sees is startup and leaves the step alone. A `lastSeenBoot` map survives `resetProviderBootState()`, so Reload, Reconnect, and a new session still report a rotation when the document actually changed. A reset that returns the same bootId leaves a live step alone. The prompt is sent once. A second send would deliver it twice if the original send had already landed, and `ProviderPageReloadedError` is not a retryable send rejection. Debate, Consult, Coding, and Roundtable stop and show a localized explanation. Brainstorm opens the existing Retry / Skip / Cancel dialog. Free still swallows the error for that provider and lets the others continue.

[#108](https://github.com/teddashh/multi-ai-chat-desktop/pull/108) 讓 workflow 認得這種換頁。`pull.ts` 匯出 `onProviderBootRotation()`：同一個 provider 出現與先前不同的 bootId 時會觸發。`waitForResponse` 在新狀態發布之前，就用 `ProviderPageReloadedError` 拒絕該 provider 的每一個等待，新頁面的 `thinking` 因此不能把不活動計時再裝回去。Host 第一次看到的 boot 是啟動，不會動到進行中的步驟。`lastSeenBoot` 在 `resetProviderBootState()` 之後仍留著，所以重新載入、重新連線與新對話在文件真的換過時仍會回報輪替；重置後若還是同一個 bootId，進行中的步驟保持原樣。提示只送出一次。若原本那次送出已經落地，再送一次會把同一則提示送兩遍，而 `ProviderPageReloadedError` 不是可重試的送出拒絕。四方辯證、多方諮詢、Coding、道理辯證會停下來，並顯示對應語系的說明。腦力激盪打開既有的重試／略過／取消對話框。自由模式維持原樣：這一家出錯時，其他家照常進行。

The same release measures composer fill. Advisory `STATUS_REPORT` fields `fill`, `fillChars`, and `fillMs` follow the existing `bulkReady` / `doneReady` pattern, so the window between a queued send and the next event is measurable. The payload carries character counts only. Prompt text stays out of it. [#108](https://github.com/teddashh/multi-ai-chat-desktop/pull/108) yielded before the fill only for `inputStrategy === 'default'`, on the reasoning that the other strategies are `async` and therefore yield. An `async` function returns a promise and still runs synchronously until its first `await`, and every strategy mutates the composer before that point. `default` (Meta) never awaits. The textarea branch of `prosemirror-paste` returns without awaiting, and its ProseMirror branch dispatches the paste before its first await. `quill-angular` (Gemini) builds one `<p>` per line and appends it before yielding. `fill: 'start'` therefore beat the mutation only for Meta. [#109](https://github.com/teddashh/multi-ai-chat-desktop/pull/109) yields unconditionally at both fill sites, the main path and the remounted-composer restore, and calls `assertCanMutate()` immediately after the yield. A challenge that arrives during the yield still aborts before any composer write. Meta stays on `inputStrategy: "default"` until a field measurement arrives.

這次同時把輸入框的填入時間量出來。`STATUS_REPORT` 增加附帶欄位 `fill`、`fillChars`、`fillMs`，比照既有的 `bulkReady`／`doneReady`，從送出排入佇列到下一筆事件之間，那段原本看不見的時間就能量測。payload 只有字數，提示文字不會進去。[#108](https://github.com/teddashh/multi-ai-chat-desktop/pull/108) 原本只在 `inputStrategy === 'default'` 時、於填入前先讓出一次，理由是其他策略寫成 `async`、應該自己會讓出。這個理由不對：`async` 只表示函式回傳 promise，本體在第一個 `await` 之前仍同步執行，而每個策略都在那之前就改了輸入框。`default`（Meta）全程沒有 `await`。`prosemirror-paste` 的 textarea 分支沒有等待就返回，ProseMirror 分支在第一個 `await` 之前就送出 paste。`quill-angular`（Gemini）會先同步為每一行建一個 `<p>` 並掛上，之後才讓出。所以 `fill: 'start'` 只有 Meta 趕在寫入之前。[#109](https://github.com/teddashh/multi-ai-chat-desktop/pull/109) 改成兩處填入都先無條件讓出：主要路徑，以及輸入框被重新掛上後的還原路徑；讓出之後立刻呼叫 `assertCanMutate()`。讓出期間若出現驗證挑戰，會在任何輸入框寫入之前中止。實地量測出現之前，Meta 維持 `inputStrategy: "default"`。

## Verification / 驗證

- CI on [#108](https://github.com/teddashh/multi-ai-chat-desktop/pull/108) and [#109](https://github.com/teddashh/multi-ai-chat-desktop/pull/109): frontend, three-platform Clippy, and CodeQL passed; the adapters job was skipped because no adapter changed. The #108 frontend run reported 752 tests. The #109 frontend run reported 755.
- [#108](https://github.com/teddashh/multi-ai-chat-desktop/pull/108) 與 [#109](https://github.com/teddashh/multi-ai-chat-desktop/pull/109) 的 CI：frontend、三平台 Clippy、CodeQL 通過；兩支都沒有改 adapter，adapters job 略過。#108 的 frontend 是 752 項測試，#109 的 frontend 是 755 項。
- Automated coverage rejects a waiter as soon as its provider's boot rotates, without waiting out the step timeout. The first boot the host sees does not reject. A later `thinking: true` cannot re-arm a rejected waiter. A reload after `resetProviderBootState()` still rejects when the bootId changed, and the same bootId after that reset does not fire a rotation. One provider rotating leaves the other provider's waiter in place. Debate, Consult, Coding, and Roundtable stop terminally. Brainstorm opens the existing Retry / Skip / Cancel dialog, and the new test settles that step with Skip. A Debate run in German shows the localized sentence. The machine string `provider page reloaded during its turn` stays out of the transcript and out of the dialog. The dialog renders the explanation in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and German. `host.provider.send` runs once for the step.
- 自動測試在 provider 的 boot 輪替時立刻拒絕等待，不會把步驟逾時等完。Host 第一次看到的 boot 不會拒絕。之後的 `thinking: true` 不能把已拒絕的等待重新計時。`resetProviderBootState()` 之後若 bootId 變了，重新載入仍會拒絕；同一個 bootId 回來則不會觸發輪替。一家輪替時，另一家的等待維持原樣。四方辯證、多方諮詢、Coding、道理辯證會直接停止。腦力激盪打開既有的重試／略過／取消對話框，這次新增的測試以略過把該步驟結束。一場德文的四方辯證會顯示該語系的說明句。內部錯誤字串 `provider page reloaded during its turn` 不會出現在逐字稿，也不會出現在對話框。對話框以英文、繁體中文、日文、德文呈現這段說明。該步驟的 `host.provider.send` 只送出一次。
- Fill-ordering tests snapshot the deferred `fill: 'start'` record at the ProseMirror paste, the ProseMirror textarea setter, and Quill's `replaceChildren`, while the composer is still untouched. Restoring the old `default`-only yield gate makes all three of those new tests fail. The existing `default` ordering test still passes under that gate. The event log keeps character counts, and a unique prompt token is absent from the title emits and from the log.
- 填入順序測試在三個寫入點拍下尚未送達的 `fill: 'start'`：ProseMirror 的 paste、ProseMirror 的 textarea setter，以及 Quill 的 `replaceChildren`。拍下時輸入框還沒被改到。把讓出恢復成只在 `default` 才執行的舊閘門，這三項新測試都會失敗。既有的 `default` 順序測試在那個閘門下仍然通過。事件日誌只留字數，用來辨識的提示字串不會出現在標題發送，也不會出現在日誌裡。

Across the 15 completed steps and that stalled step there are zero false failures. Meta's three startup rotations at 04:44:59, 04:45:09, and 04:45:15 land before any step opens. ChatGPT's two rotations at 05:03:35 and 05:03:38 land after its last step finished. This is a replay of a recorded trace. A live retest of the `v1.9.5` fixes has not been run.

- Known gap: whether Meta's `execCommand('insertText', …)` on a very large prompt is what wedged the renderer is still unproven. The trouble scaled with prompt size, which is circumstantial. The new fill markers exist so a later field measurement can settle it. Meta stays on `inputStrategy: "default"` until that measurement arrives.
- 已知缺口：Meta 在很長的提示上呼叫 `execCommand('insertText', …)` 是否就是把 renderer 卡住的原因，仍然沒有證明。問題隨著提示變長而變重，這只是旁證。新的 fill 標記是為了以後用實地量測把這件事定下來。那份量測出現之前，Meta 維持 `inputStrategy: "default"`。
- Known gap: the new failure triggers on a document replacement, detected as a new bootId. If a provider's engine goes silent or wedges and its page is never replaced, that step still waits out the 10-minute inactivity window and the 60-minute absolute cap, exactly as before. The composer-fill markers are what would let a later bundle tell those two cases apart.
- 已知缺口：新的失敗在文件被換掉時觸發，依據是新的 bootId。provider 的 engine 若靜默或卡住，而頁面始終沒有被換掉，這一步仍會等到 10 分鐘不活動窗口和 60 分鐘絕對上限，與以前相同。之後的 bundle 要分辨這兩種情況，靠的是輸入框的 fill 標記。
- The open step now fails when its provider page is replaced, where that replacement previously looked like a stall. A provider whose page flaps mid-step surfaces as a visible error.
- 進行中的步驟在 provider 頁面被換掉時現在會失敗；同樣的替換以前看起來只是停住。頁面若在步驟中途一再重載，畫面上會看到錯誤。
